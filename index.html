How I lead

  • Start with the person on the hardest day.A tired driver at night. A fleet manager after an incident. If the product works for them then, it works.
  • Let evidence settle arguments.Research and data decide what users need, not the loudest opinion in the room, including mine.
  • Teach design to the whole company.In an engineering-led company, everyone should ask how it feels from the user's side, even when design isn't in the room.
  • Build the team and the system.Hire well, mentor closely, and set up the design system and rituals that let a small team ship consistently.
  • Hold the whole experience.Device, app, dashboard, brand, sales demo and support are one product to the customer, so I design them as one.
