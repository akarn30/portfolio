Designing Safety-Critical Driver Warning Systems

How we designed in-cab acoustic and visual alerts for Sapience CoPilot AI™ that tired drivers trust at 3 AM instead of turning off.

The Challenge

In-cab driver monitoring systems frequently fail not because the AI misses drowsiness, but because drivers find constant loud beeps intolerable and cover the sensors or disable power.

[ Media Placeholder: In-Cab Alert Hardware & UI ]

Design Principles

1. Alert the driver first, not the fleet manager.
2. Single clear signal, delivered only when fatigue threshold is breached.
3. Graduated tone frequency based on verified eye-closure duration.
