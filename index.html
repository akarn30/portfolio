Animesh Singh is a design leader in Bengaluru. He leads design for AI that watches the road as Chief Experience Officer at Sapience Automata. Before that he designed for millions of small businesses at Khatabook and built a fintech brand at Instamojo.

Driver monitoring and ADAS for commercial fleets and carmakers. A camera in the cab reads the road and the driver's attention, and speaks up before something goes wrong. He designs for three people at once: the driver, who has under a second; the fleet manager, who needs to know who to call today; and the carmaker, who needs it to belong in their vehicle.
Joined as VP of Marketing and Product Design in August 2024, promoted to CXO within twelve months. Leads product design, UX, brand, marketing and customer experience, and a team of six designers.
2020 to 2023. Core and growth design for a bookkeeping app used by over 10 million merchants, many of them first-time smartphone users reading in their own language.
2017 to 2020. Brand and product communication for a payments platform serving over 1.5 million small businesses. Trained at Ogilvy & Mather. Studied visual communication at NIFT Kolkata.

He believes design is how a company decides what matters to people, and then makes it obvious. He is looking to lead a design team, from January 2027.

● approach

  1. Start with the person on the hardest day. A tired driver at night. If it works then, it works.
  2. Let evidence settle the argument. Not the loudest voice in the room, including his.
  3. Teach design to the whole company. Everyone asks how it feels, even when design isn't there.
  4. Build the team and the system. Hire well, mentor closely, keep the rituals small.
  5. Hold the whole experience. Device, app, dashboard and support are one product.
